# Bitstream Hardware Validation Checklist

**Target Device:** Samsung Galaxy Tab S10 FE (`SM-X526B`, Android 16 / API 36, `arm64-v8a`)
**Target USB DAC:** JadeAudio / FiiO JA11 (`VID 0x3699`, `PID 0x0141`, UAC 2.0, Asynchronous 0x04 / Adaptive 0x08, Interface 1 Alt 1–2, Endpoint `0x02` ISO OUT)
**Validation Date:** 2026-11-05

## Checklist & Results

| # | Category | Test Case | Result | Notes / Evidence |
|---|----------|-----------|--------|------------------|
| 1 | USB Enumeration & Permission | Detect attached DAC, request permission, persist remembered device key across restarts, and open `UsbDeviceConnection.getFileDescriptor()` | **PASS** | `UsbDeviceManager` + `AndroidUsbDeviceSource` detect `JadeAudio JA11` (`3699:0141`), parse the 409-byte raw descriptor (`UAC 2.0`, Clock Source `ID 9`, Feature Unit `ID 10`), and query 8 discrete sample rates (`44.1`, `48`, `88.2`, `96`, `176.4`, `192`, `352.8`, `384` kHz). |
| 2 | Sample Rates & Bit Depths | Native sample rate switching (`SET_CUR CS_SAM_FREQ_CONTROL`) and PCM packing across 16/24/32-bit and 44.1–384 kHz | **PASS** | Verified bit-exact `pack_pcm` in `NativeTests` (16-in-16, 16-in-24, 16-in-32, 24-in-24, 24-in-32 left-justified `s << 8` with LSB `0x00`, 32-in-32) and verified on-device playback at `44.1 kHz / 16-bit`, `96 kHz / 24-bit`, and `192 kHz / 24-bit` (`Bit-perfect • MD5 verified • 0 underruns`). |
| 3 | Gapless & Format Transitions | Gapless pre-enqueue within 10 s of track end when `(sampleRate, bitDepth, channels)` match; clean stream reopen on format change | **PASS** | Verified in `PlaybackEngineTest` (`gapless is enqueued when rate depth and channels match within 10s of end`, `gapless is requested only when format matches`) and `NativeAudioBackend` `nativeEnqueueNext` / `nativeSetSampleRate`. |
| 4 | Transport Controls | Play, pause, resume, frame-accurate seek (`FLAC__stream_decoder_seek_absolute`), next, previous, seeded shuffle with history, repeat (`OFF`, `ALL`, `ONE`) | **PASS** | Verified in `PlaybackEngineTest`, `NowPlayingViewModelTest`, `QueueViewModelTest`, and on-device Now Playing / MiniPlayer / QueueSheet controls. |
| 5 | Background & MediaSession | Foreground `PlaybackService` (`foregroundServiceType="mediaPlayback"`), `MediaSession`, audio focus (`AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN`, pause on loss, never duck), notification & lockscreen transport | **PASS** | Verified in `BitstreamPlayerTest` (`play pause seek next reflect engine state`) and `PlaybackService` on `SM-X526B`. |
| 6 | Volume & Bit-Perfect Badge | UAC2 Feature Unit hardware volume (`SET_CUR 0x21/0x01`) preserves `Bit-perfect` badge; software volume (`Q1.30` with TPDF dither) demotes path badge to `Software volume` and relabels integrity to `Decode verified` | **PASS** | Verified in `StatusChipTest`, `PlaybackEngineTest`, and on-device on `SM-X526B` + JA11 (`Software volume • Decode verified • 0 underruns`). |
| 7 | FLAC MD5 Integrity | `MD5 verified` on full decode (`md5State == 1`), `Partial (seeked)` after seek, `Unavailable` when `STREAMINFO` MD5 is zero, `Failed` on corrupted stream | **PASS** | Verified in `NativeTests.FlacDecoder` (16/44.1, 24/96, 24/192, seek, and corrupted frame tests) and on-device playback. |
| 8 | USB Detach & Reconnect | Unplugging DAC mid-track stops stream, releases wakelock, sets `PAUSED`, keeps queue/position, and posts notice; replugging restores `BitPerfectPath` and offers resume without auto-playing | **PASS** | Verified in `ErrorHandlingTest` (`detach pauses and keeps the queue`, `reconnect does not auto-play without user action`) and `PlaybackService` status notification. |
| 9 | Unsupported Format & Decode Errors | Mixed-rate queue with unsupported sample rate or corrupted track skips to next playable track and records a user-facing notice | **PASS** | Verified in `PlaybackEngineTest` (`auto-advance skips an unsupported rate with a notice`, `decode failure advances and reports`) and `ErrorHandlingTest` (`native error codes map to typed PlaybackError and user message`). |
| 10 | Responsive Layouts & Rotation | Compact (`< 600 dp`) uses `NavigationBar` and stacked Now Playing; Medium/Expanded (`>= 600 dp`) uses `NavigationRail`, `ListDetailPaneScaffold`, side-by-side Now Playing, and supporting `QueuePane`; rotation preserves state | **PASS** | Verified on `SM-X526B` in `AdaptiveLayoutTest` (`compact_width_shows_bottom_navigation`, `expanded_width_shows_navigation_rail`) and portrait/landscape rotation checks. |
| 11 | Battery & Wakelock Hygiene | Partial wakelock (`bitstream:playback`) acquired only during `OPENING`/`READY` and released immediately on `PAUSED`/`IDLE`/`ENDED`/`NO_DAC` | **PASS** | Verified via `adb shell dumpsys power` on `SM-X526B` — no `bitstream:playback` wakelock held while idle or paused. |
| 12 | UAC 1.0 Coverage | UAC 1.0 descriptor parsing (`FORMAT_TYPE_I`, discrete rates, `bmAttributes & 0x0C`) | **PASS (Fixture)** | Covered by `UacDescriptorParserTest` with synthetic UAC1 descriptor fixture; marked best-effort per spec §15 (no physical UAC1 hardware). |
